The MIT licence at the project root covers the original director instructions, original design specification, validation utilities and original design-lab composition. It does not relicense linked templates, software, fonts, images, audio or third-party methods/documents. No current personal MotionGit package, original footage, repository checkout or credentials are included.

- Adu: [adu-motion-video](https://github.com/adunext/adu-motion-video), legacy commit `4a52ae2cff542cfb0bfcf6e93e5120c55d0110cf`, MIT code. Public demonstrations and third-party footage/music have separate restrictions. Thirteen current free MotionGit pages explicitly state MIT code; fifteen gated pages' complete licences remain unverified. The research catalog links sources, it is not a substitute template distribution.

Linked repositories without licences are not treated as permission to redistribute.

Fonts are not sold separately.

The Python adapter is written for this project with exact-ID checks, bounded downloads and evidence output; the original MIT notice is retained in `skill/references/licenses/douyin-transcriber-MIT.txt`. - Optional video analysis uses faster-whisper / CTranslate2, PyAV, RapidOCR, ONNX Runtime and OpenCV. Each dependency and model retains its own licence. Installed packages/model weights are not bundled; the tested compatibility versions are in `skill/scripts/requirements-video-reader.txt`.

Third-party screenshots and sampled videos used for private research are kept outside the public distribution. Preview images and videos in `docs/` are rendered from the original design-lab project, using the bundled OFL fonts.
